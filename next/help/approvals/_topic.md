Approvals are where people decide: a run's results, a step in the middle of a run, a document's values, or a request
that walks an approval workflow. Review gathers everything waiting for you in one list with one count; approval workflows
lay out who approves, what is checked and what runs; teams and managers say who decides.

## Questions
### Why can't I approve my own request?
No one approves their own request: someone else decides it, as the workflow names. See
[Who decides](#apr-who-decides).

### Why was an item skipped when I approved several at once?
Each item is decided by the rules of a single decision; the panel says why each one was skipped, such as *The pipeline
requires individual review*. See [Decide many at once](#apr-bulk--why-an-item-is-skipped).

### What happens when I reject an Approval step?
The run fails in a moment, and its message carries your reason. See
[Approval steps inside a run](#apr-approval-steps--decide-it).

### What does the number beside Review mean?
Everything waiting for your decision: approvals, run results and documents, counted from the list **All waiting** shows.
See [Review](#apr-review-home--the-badge).

### If I publish a new version, what happens to requests already running?
They keep the version they started on; new requests use the new one. See
[Design an approval workflow](#apr-workflows--publish-versions-and-test-runs).

### Who may change a team's members?
Its lead, or a workspace administrator; an administrator names the leads. See [Who decides](#apr-who-decides--teams).
