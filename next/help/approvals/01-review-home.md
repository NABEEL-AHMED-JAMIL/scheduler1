Everything that waits for a person is in one place: **Run & review › Review**. Approval steps and tasks of workflows, runs
whose results wait for a review, and documents with values to check are listed together, overdue first, then the longest
waiting, with one count beside **Review** in the menu.

![Review › All waiting](apr-review-all "Review › All waiting: an approval task and two documents, each with what it is, where it came from and whom it waits on")

## The four tabs
| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **All waiting** | Everything below, together, in one list |
| **Approvals** | Approval steps and tasks for you or your teams, run reviews among them, the soonest due first (see [Review a run's results](#apr-run-review)) |
| **Runs** | Runs whose results wait for a review, newest first |
| **Documents** | Documents with values to check beside the page image (see [Review a document](#doc-review)) |

Each tab has its own page access, so a reviewer sees only the kinds their profile lets them decide.

## Work through All waiting
1. Open **Run & review › Review**. **All waiting** opens first.
2. Narrow it by kind (**All**, **Approvals**, **Runs**, **Documents**, each with its count), by **Waits on** (**Anyone**,
   **You**, **Your teams**, **Not claimed**), or with **Search what, from, who**.
3. Each row says its **Type**, **What** it is (with a line under it, such as *2 of 13 values to check*), where it is
   **From** (the workflow, the pipeline, the document type), **Waiting since**, whom it **Waits on**, and **Due / age**
   (*Due in 21 h*, *Overdue · was due …*, *Waiting 3 h*).
4. Click a row to read it in the side panel: what it is, what to check, and **Approve…** or **Reject…** when you may
   decide it there; or open its own page with **Open in Approvals**, **Open the run** or **Open the document**.
5. To decide several at once, tick them and choose **Approve** or **Reject** in the bar (see
   [Decide many at once](#apr-bulk)).

> **Note:** Some rows cannot be decided from the list, and say why: your own request (someone else decides it), a run
> whose pipeline requires individual review (open it to decide), a task step (open it to do it), or a document someone
> else has claimed.

## The badge
The number beside **Review** in the menu, and Home's *N waiting for your review*, count everything in **All waiting** for
you. It is read again whenever a new notification arrives (a new task, a reminder, an escalation) and every two minutes
while the console is open, so it stays current without a refresh.

## Example
Dr. Anika Patel opens **Review** at the start of her shift. **All waiting** shows two referral documents with values to
check from the night's intake, and the care plan waiting for her approval in the *Patient journey*. She filters **Waits on**
to **You**, decides the care plan first because it is due soonest, and leaves the documents to the HIM team.

## Limits
| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| What you see | Only what your page access and your teams let you decide |
| Your own requests | Listed, never decided by you |
