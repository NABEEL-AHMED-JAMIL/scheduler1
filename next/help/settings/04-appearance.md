The console comes in six themes. Each person can pick the one they like; a workspace administrator can set the one
everyone else opens in.

## The themes
| Theme | What it is |
|---|---|
| **System** | Light or Dark, as your device is set |
| **Light** | The console's own, on white |
| **Dark** | Neutral dark |
| **Ocean** | Calm blue and teal on light |
| **Midnight** | Deep navy with teal accents |
| **High contrast** | Black on white, strongest edges |

## Pick your own theme
- In the top bar, open the theme button (the sun or moon) and pick one. It changes at once.
- Or open **Your profile** from the account menu and pick it under **Theme**. When the workspace has a default, the
  profile says so, with **Use the workspace default** to go back to it.

Your choice stays yours, whatever the workspace's default is.

## Set the workspace's default
1. Open **Administration & billing › Administration › Appearance**.
2. Under **Default theme**, pick one. **System (no default)** leaves each device's own light or dark.
3. The page says *Not saved yet* and which theme; **Undo** puts it back.
4. Choose **Save default**.

People who have chosen a theme keep theirs; everyone else opens in the new default from their next sign-in.

## Dashboards' chart themes
Dashboards' charts follow the console's theme, light or dark. A board's editor can also give the whole board a chart
theme of its own, and a widget can override it in its **Chart settings**. Their colours are held to a 3 : 1 contrast
against the card in light and dark. See [Build and edit a dashboard](#dash-build--the-boards-chart-theme).

## Example
Summit Valley Health System's default is **Light**, the theme of its printed reports. The night charge nurses on
Ward 4B pick **Dark** for themselves in the top bar, and Dr. Okafor picks **High contrast** on his profile. Neither
choice changes anyone else's.
