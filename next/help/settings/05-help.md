Help is in three places: this **help centre** for everyone who uses the console, the **Developer portal** for the
developers who connect your systems, and the people to ask when neither answers.

## Open the help centre
1. Open the account menu (your name, top right) and choose **Setup guide**. The help centre opens.
2. Type in the search box: every article is searched, its steps included. **Clear search** goes back.
3. Or **Browse by topic**: you see the topics for the pages you can open. Each topic starts with **Start here**, its
   common questions, and its articles in order.
4. **Popular how-tos** and **What's new** are on the front page; **Read the whole guide** puts every article on one
   page, to read through or print.
5. **Back to console** returns you to where you were.

![The help centre](set-help "The help centre: search every article, and browse the topics for the pages you can open")

> **Tip:** Every article has its own address (for example `/docs#set-alerts`). Copy it from your browser to point a
> colleague straight to the steps.

## For developers: the Developer portal
The API's guides, its reference with a call to copy for each, samples in curl, Python and Node.js, the event and
problem types, the changelog and the downloads are in **Administration & billing › Developers › Developer portal**.
See [The Developer portal](#dev-portal).

## When the help does not answer
- **Something you cannot open or change** (a page, a tab, a button greyed out): ask your workspace administrator.
  Page access, teams and roles are theirs.
- **A run that failed**: open it from **Run & review › Runs › Failed**; the failing step says why, and most failures
  can be retried from that step.
- **A bill, a limit, the sandbox, the billing time zone, a model or a service**: your workspace administrator asks
  your account team.
- **Something that looks broken**: note the page's address, what you did and when (to the minute, in your time zone),
  and send it to your workspace administrator. A screenshot helps, as long as it shows no patient or personal data.

> **Privacy:** Never paste a secret, a password or a patient's details into a support message.

## Example
Rosa Alvarez, charge nurse at Summit Valley Health System, cannot find the **Approve** button on a care plan. She
searches the help centre for *approve*, reads that her page access decides what she may decide, and asks the
workspace administrator, who adds her to the right team.
