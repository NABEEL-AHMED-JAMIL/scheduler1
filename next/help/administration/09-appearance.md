**Appearance** sets the theme your workspace's people open the console in until they choose their own. Anyone can still pick another theme in the top bar or on **Your profile**; their own choice always wins.

![Administration › Appearance](adm-appearance "Default theme: the themes, the one saved now, and Save default")

## Set the default theme
1. Open **Administration › Appearance**.
2. Under **Default theme**, pick **System (no default)**, **Light**, **Dark**, **Ocean**, **Midnight** or **High contrast**. The line under the title says what is saved now.
3. Choose **Save default**. **Undo** goes back to the saved one before you save.

People who have not chosen a theme open in the new default from their next sign-in. People who have chosen one keep theirs; on **Your profile** they see the workspace's default and can choose **Use the workspace default**.

## The themes
| Theme | What it is |
|---|---|
| System | Light or Dark, as each person's device is set |
| Light | The console's own, on white |
| Dark | Neutral dark |
| Ocean | Calm blue and teal on light |
| Midnight | Deep navy with teal accents |
| High contrast | Black on white, strongest edges |

> **Tip:** High contrast helps people with low vision and on bright ward screens; suggest it rather than making it everyone's default.

## Example
Summit Valley's default theme is Light, so printed screenshots and shared screens look the same for everyone. The night-shift nurses on Ward 4B choose Midnight for themselves.
