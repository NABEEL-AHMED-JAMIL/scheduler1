When you are away, an **out of office** lets a colleague (your stand-in) act on the approval and workflow tasks assigned to you, for a set period. Every decision they make records both of you.

## How decisions show
A decision made by a stand-in appears in the request's history with both names, for example:

```A request's history
Care plan: approved by Dr. Minh Nguyen (for Dr. Anika Patel)
```

So the record always says who decided and on whose behalf.

## The rules
- An out of office runs from a start to an end in the future, **at most 90 days**. For longer, reassign the work instead.
- The stand-in must be an active person of the same workspace, and not yourself.
- You set your own; only an administrator sets one for someone else.
- One at a time: if one already covers those dates, cancel it first.
- It does not chain: your stand-in's own stand-in cannot act for you.
- Cancelling stops it at once. It is never deleted, so the history can always say who stood in for whom. Only the person away, or an administrator, can cancel it.

> **Note:** There is no page in the console for setting an out of office yet. For a single task, use **Pass on** in **Review › Approvals** instead: choose the task, **Pass on**, pick the person under **Pass it to**, add a note if you like, and choose **Pass on**.

## Other ways to keep work moving
- Send approvals to a [team](#adm-teams) rather than one person, so anyone in it can decide.
- Give each person a **Manager** on their page: overdue tasks escalate to them.

## Example
Dr. Anika Patel is at a conference for three days. Her out of office names Dr. Minh Nguyen. The care plan for a ward patient reaches Dr. Patel's list; Dr. Nguyen approves it, and its history reads "approved by Dr. Minh Nguyen (for Dr. Anika Patel)".
