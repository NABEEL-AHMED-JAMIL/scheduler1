## Questions
### What is the difference between a workspace administrator and a member?
An administrator opens every page and changes people, connections, pipelines and settings; a member opens the pages on their profile. See [The two roles](#adm-people--the-two-roles).

### Someone forgot their password. How do I reset it?
Choose **Reset password** in their row on **Administration › Users**; they choose their own at their next sign-in. See [Reset someone's password](#adm-reset-remove--reset-someones-password).

### Should I deactivate or delete someone who left?
Deactivate: it keeps their work and history and can be undone. See [Remove someone's access](#adm-reset-remove--remove-someones-access).

### Why does a new member see every page?
The workspace has no access profiles yet, or no default. See [How it works](#adm-access-profiles--how-it-works).

### Can a team lead give their team access without being an administrator?
Yes, as a delegate, within limits and for a set time. See [Delegates](#adm-delegates).

### Do teams decide which pages people see?
No. Teams decide where work goes; page access decides what people open. See [Teams](#adm-teams).

### How do I give a colleague my approvals while I am away?
With an out of office, or pass one task on with **Pass on**. See [Out of office](#adm-out-of-office).
