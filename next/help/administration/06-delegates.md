A **delegate** is a workspace member who may grant access to other members without being an administrator: a director who opens their team's pages, or HR who gives new starters the HR or Viewer pages. The delegation lasts a set time and has firm limits, and everything a delegate does is recorded.

![Delegates on Page access](adm-delegates "Delegates: who may grant, to whom, for how long, and why")

## Make someone a delegate
1. Open **Administration › Page access**, scroll to **Grant pages and tabs** and choose **Delegates**.
2. Under **Member who may grant**, pick the member.
3. Under **To whom**, choose **Every member** or **Only people I choose**, and tick the people.
4. Under **For**, choose **90 days** (the default), **30 days**, **180 days** or **A year**.
5. Tick **May move people between existing profiles** if they may also change which profile someone holds.
6. Type **Why**. The access review reads it.
7. Choose **Delegate**.

The delegation appears in the list with its **Until** date and state **Active**. **End** stops it at once; when its time runs out it reads **Expired**.

## The limits
A delegate:
- grants only pages and tabs they hold themselves;
- never grants to an administrator or to themselves;
- never creates, changes or deletes profiles (at most moves people between existing ones, if allowed);
- reaches only the people the delegation names.

## What the delegate sees
A delegate sees **Administration › Page access** in their menu, showing only **Grant access for the people you manage**, a line saying who delegated it and until when, and **A person** and **Changes**. They grant as an administrator does: see [Give a person only some tabs](#adm-some-tabs).

## Every change is recorded
**Changes** lists every access change in the workspace, newest first: **When**, **Who** made it, **What** (for example **Opened a tab**, **Withheld a page**, **Moved to a profile**, **Back to their profile**, **Delegated access**, **Ended a delegation**, **Changed a profile's two-factor rule**), the **Person**, the **Page or tab**, **Until** and **Why**. A delegate's own **Changes** shows the changes they made.

![The change log](adm-changes "Changes: every access change, who made it and why")

## Example
At Summit Valley, Owen Wright in HR is a delegate for 90 days, for every member, and may move people between existing profiles. His reason: "HR grants HR and Viewer pages to the HR and Facilities teams". When a new facilities coordinator starts, Owen moves her onto the Viewer profile himself; the change shows under **Changes** with his name and reason.
