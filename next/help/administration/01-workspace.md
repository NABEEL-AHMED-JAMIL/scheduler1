A **workspace** is your organisation's own space in the console. It holds everything else: its people and their access, connections, pipelines, schedules, runs, files, reports, dashboards, settings and bills. Nothing crosses from one workspace to another, and a person belongs to exactly one.

Your workspace's administrators run it from **Administration & billing › Administration**, whose tabs are **Users**, **Teams**, **Page access**, **Sign-in security**, **Data policies** and **Appearance** (and **Activity** when our team works in it).

## Its name
The workspace's name shows on **Your profile** (under **Workspace**), on **Administration › Users** and in the bar our staff see while they work in it. It is set when the workspace is created; to change it, ask the platform's team.

## Its time zones
- **Times on screen** are shown in each person's own device time zone, on the 24-hour clock.
- **Billing days and months** are counted in the workspace's **billing time zone** (America/Chicago unless the platform's team set another). **Billing › Cost & usage** shows it as **Days in …**. A change takes effect from the next month that has not started, never the month under way, and issued invoices never change.
- **Retention** runs its daily pass after 02:00 Chicago time.

## Its default theme
People who have not chosen a theme of their own open the console in the workspace's default theme. Set it under [Appearance](#adm-appearance).

## Self-managed or managed by our team
A workspace is built either by its own administrators or by our team for you.

| | Self-managed | Managed by our team |
|---|---|---|
| Who builds pipelines, schedules, connections and forms | Your administrators | Our team |
| What your administrators can still do | Everything | Manage people and access, run pipelines (**Run now**), review and approve, read results and bills |
| Build screens | Editable | Read-only, with the note **Managed by our team** |
| Our team's work | Only in a recorded managed session | In a recorded managed session |
| **Administration › Activity** | Appears once our team has acted in the workspace | Always there |

![The note on a build screen in a managed workspace](adm-managed-banner "Managed by our team: the build screens are read-only; ask your account team for a change")

The platform's team switches a workspace between the two. Whichever it is, everything our staff change or open in your workspace is recorded: see [The managed service](#sec-managed).

> **Tip:** Give the workspace at least two administrators, so nobody is locked out when one is away.

## Example
Summit Valley Health System is a self-managed workspace with two administrators: the Summit Valley admin builds the pipelines and Jordan Reyes, the operations director, approves report assets and covers when the first is away. Its billing time zone is America/Chicago and its default theme is Light.
