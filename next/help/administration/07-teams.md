A **team** is a group of people who work together: Physicians, Ward 4B nursing, Pharmacy, Finance controls. Work can be sent to a team instead of to one person, so whoever in the team is free picks it up. A person can be in many teams.

Teams are used for:
- **approval workflows**: a step can be assigned to a team;
- **run reviews** and **workflow tasks** waiting for the team;
- **alerts**: an alert rule can notify a team;
- **Report Studio**: the reviewers who approve reports and the publishers of templates can be a team.

Teams open no pages. What a person may open is their page access.

![Administration › Teams](adm-teams "Teams: each team's lead, members and where it is used")

## Make a team and name its lead
1. Open **Administration › Teams** and choose **New team**.
2. Type its **Name** (for example Wound care nurses) and, if you like, a **Description**. Choose **Make team**.
3. In the team's panel, under **Add a person**, choose someone, pick **Member** or **Lead**, and choose **Add**. Repeat for each person.
4. To make an existing member the lead, change **Member** to **Lead** beside their name.

![A team's panel](adm-team-panel "The team's panel: about, members with Member or Lead, and where it is used")

## Leads and members
- A **lead** adds and removes the team's members. They reach the team from **Review › Approvals** (**My teams**, then **See who is in your teams**).
- Only an administrator names a team's leads, renames it or archives it.
- A team with **No lead** is changed by administrators only.

## Find and read teams
The tiles count **Teams**, **People in teams**, **Leads** and teams **Without a lead**. Search by team, description or person, or filter by lead, member and state. **Used by** shows how many workflows, approvals, alerts and Report Studio settings send work to the team; **Where it is used** in the panel names them, each a link.

You can also add a person to a team from their own page on **Administration › Users** (**Teams**, then **Add to a team…**).

## Archive a team
In the team's panel choose **Archive team**. Nothing new goes to an archived team, and its history keeps its name. Show archived teams with the state filter.

> **Tip:** Before archiving, check **Where it is used**: a workflow step or alert that names the team will have nobody to send to.

## Example
Summit Valley's Physicians team has Dr. Anika Patel (lead), Dr. Minh Nguyen and Dr. Chidi Okafor. **Where it is used** shows the Patient journey's care-plan and discharge approvals and the Report Studio reviewers, so either doctor can approve a discharge summary when Dr. Patel is with a patient.
