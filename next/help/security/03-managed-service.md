Some customers have our team build their pipelines, schedules and connections for them. Such a workspace is **managed by our team**; the others are **self-managed**. The platform's team switches a workspace between the two, and can switch it back.

## What changes in a managed workspace
- The build screens (pipelines, connections, forms, the Task Registry and others) are read-only for your own people, with the note **Managed by our team**: "Our team builds and changes this workspace's …, so they are read-only here. Contact your account team to request a change."
- Your administrators still manage **people and access**, sign-in rules and data policies, run pipelines with **Run now**, review and approve, and read every result and bill.
- Our team builds in a recorded managed session.
- **Administration › Activity** is always on your administrators' menu.

![The note on a build screen](adm-managed-banner "In a managed workspace the build screens are read-only for your own people")

In a self-managed workspace your administrators build everything themselves. **Activity** appears there too once our team has acted in the workspace, and stays, so a workspace that goes back to self-managed keeps its history.

## Our team's activity
**Administration › Activity** (**Our team's activity**) lists every change our team made in your workspace and every data they opened, exported or downloaded, newest first.

![Our team's activity](sec-team-activity "Our team's activity: when, who, what they changed or read")

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **When** | Date and time, to the second |
| **Staff member** | Who, with their sign-in address |
| **Change or read** | What they did. **Read** marks data they opened, exported or downloaded; **Builder** marks a change to what the workspace builds |
| **Service** | The part of the platform that recorded it |

Filter by **Every staff member** and by changes or reads; **Load older** goes further back.

## Example
For one week Summit Valley is managed while our team builds its population-health pipelines. Its administrators keep running the admission pipeline with **Run now** and approving discharges; each evening Jordan Reyes reads **Our team's activity** to see what was built and which run outputs our staff opened while testing.
