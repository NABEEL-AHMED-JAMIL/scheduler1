The console records who did what as it happens. There is no single audit page: each record lives next to the thing it is about, where the people who look after that thing will find it.

## Where each record is
| What | Where | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-ins | **Administration › Sign-in security › Sign-in history**; each person's own on **My security** | When, who, outcome, method (password, code, recovery code, single sign-on), reason, address, device. **Export CSV** |
| A person's account | Their page on **Administration › Users** › **Activity** | Recent sign-ins and account changes, who last edited it |
| Access changes | **Administration › Page access › Changes** | Every page or tab opened or withheld, profile moves, delegations, two-factor rule changes: when, who, for whom, until when, why |
| Approvals and reviews | The request's history in **Review**; each run's and document's own page | Who approved, rejected or asked for changes, with their comment; a stand-in shows as "(for …)"; a bulk decision is recorded on every item |
| Reports | A report's **Activity** tab in **Reports** | Created, generation requested, version made, failed, downloaded, archived |
| Retention | **Data policies › Retention › History** | Every pass, what it removed; every legal hold placed and released |
| Our staff | **Administration › Activity** | Every change our team made, and every data they opened, exported or downloaded |
| File downloads and previews | Kept as audit records | Never removed before the platform's minimum of 2,190 days |

## Find out who changed someone's access
1. Open **Administration › Page access**, scroll to **Grant pages and tabs** and choose **Changes**.
2. Read down to the person: **Who** made the change, **What** it was, the **Page or tab**, **Until** and **Why**.

A delegate's changes show with the delegate's name, and the delegation itself (**Delegated access**, **Ended a delegation**) with the administrator's.

## Find out who approved something
Open the item in **Review** (or the run or document) and read its history: each step, who decided, when, and their comment.

## Find out who downloaded a report
Open the report in **Reports › Library** and choose its **Activity** tab. Each download is listed with its time.

> **Tip:** For a periodic access review, export **Sign-in history** and read **Changes** for the period; the reasons typed with each grant and delegation are there for that purpose.

## Example
Summit Valley's quarterly access review: Jordan Reyes exports the sign-in history for the quarter, reads **Changes** for every tab opened for one person (Dr. Patel's week of ward cover, with its reason and its end date), and checks **Our team's activity** for the week the workspace was managed.
