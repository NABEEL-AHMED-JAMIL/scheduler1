The console uses AI models to read documents, describe images, summarise, draft and answer questions. You decide which models may see which data, a person checks what a model says before it is used, and the AI Assistant asks before it changes anything.

## Which models see your data
- Models run through your workspace's **model connections** (**Settings › Model connections**). A connection to a model running on your own machines is **local**; others are hosted.
- Your **data policy** gives each level of data a model rule: **Local only**, **BAA-signed hosted, or local**, or **Any model**. **Sensitive** data starts as **Local only**: it never goes to a hosted provider until an administrator says otherwise.
- A call's level is the highest level known of it: the prompt's declared level, and in a tool run every API collection it has read. A call is internal when nothing says otherwise.

See [Which models may see which data](#ai-data-levels) and [Your models](#ai-models).

## Every model answer is a DRAFT
A model can be wrong. In the console a model's answer is a draft for a person to check:
- a pipeline can hold its results for review, so a person approves or rejects them in **Review** before they are used;
- documents read with low confidence go to **Review › Documents**;
- a report is approved and signed by a person.

> **Warning:** Do not let a model's answer reach a patient, a payment or a record without a person approving it.

## What is sent to a model
Only what the step or prompt sends: its instructions and the fields or files it is given. Send only the fields the task needs; the **Minimum-fields warning** reminds whoever builds a step to do so. Columns masked for a person stay masked in Ask your data and on the dashboards they open.

## What the AI Assistant may change
The AI Assistant can design and run pipelines for you. Its **write tools** (running pipelines, creating PDFs, saving or deleting files, calling APIs that change data) are set per data level under **AI write tools**, off for Sensitive by default, and it still asks before it uses one.

## Example
At Summit Valley every model is local, and Sensitive stays **Local only**. The wound-photo step describes each photo as a DRAFT; Sam Harper, the wound specialist, approves or corrects every measurement in **Review** before it reaches the patient's report.
