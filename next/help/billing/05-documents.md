**Billing documents** keeps every billing PDF of the workspace together: invoices, receipts, payment slips, credit
notes and statements. Pick one on the left to read it on the right.

## Find a document
1. Open **Administration & billing › Billing › Billing documents**.
2. The figures at the top count the year's **Documents**, **Invoices** (and how much was invoiced), **Receipts** (and
   how much was received) and **Payment slips**.
3. Narrow the list with **Find a number, file, person**, a kind (**All kinds**, or one) and the **Year**.
4. Choose a document. It opens beside the list, page by page, with **Download**; ⋯ › **Open in a new tab** shows it on
   its own.

## Statements
A **statement** lists what was issued in its range, oldest first, in your billing days: each invoice, each payment
against it, each credit note, then what was **Invoiced**, **Credited** and **Paid** (net of refunds), the **Balance
open**, and any credit owed to you.

To make one, pick the **Year** and choose **Statement 2026** (the button names the year) at the top of **Billing
documents**, or, on **Invoices**, ⋯ › **Statement for this year**. It is added to the list.

## Check that a receipt is genuine
Receipts are signed with the platform's certificate.

1. Choose **Verify a receipt** at the top of the page, and give it the receipt's PDF.
2. The console says whether the file is a receipt this platform issued, unchanged since it was signed.

An open receipt has **Verify signature** above it, which checks its digital signature, hash and certificate on the
spot.

## Example
For its year-end audit, Summit Valley Health System's controller Claire Martin makes the statement for 2026: the
September invoice, two bank payments against it and the credit note, with a balance open of zero. She downloads it
with the two signed receipts and checks one with **Verify a receipt** before sending the pack to the auditors.
