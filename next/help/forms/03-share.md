A form is shared inside its workspace: every member whose page access includes **Forms** sees its Active forms and fills
them in, signed in as themselves, so each submission says who sent it. Nothing about a form is public unless an
administrator deliberately makes a link.

## Fill in a form inside the workspace
1. Open **Get data in › Forms**. An Active form's name is a link; or choose **Fill in** in its row menu.
2. Answer the fields. Required ones are marked with an asterisk; a field's help text is under it.
3. For a **Signature**, sign inside the box and choose **Use this signature** (**Clear** starts again).
4. Choose **Submit**. The page says **Submitted**; **Fill in another** starts a fresh one.

To send a colleague straight to it, copy the address of the form's page: it opens only for a signed-in member of your
workspace whose page access includes Forms.

## Share by link (administrators)
For people who should fill in one form and see nothing else (a front-desk tablet, say), an administrator can make a
link. It is in the form's **Settings** tab, under **Share by link**, after the form has been saved.

1. Tick **Sharing by link is off for this workspace**; it then says **on**. While it is off, no link of this workspace opens anything;
   turning it off again stops every link at once.
2. Give the link a **Label**, such as `Front desk tablet`.
3. Pick how long it **Works for**: 1, 7, 14, 30 or 90 days.
4. Pick how many **Submissions** it takes: **No cap**, **One (single use)**, 10 or 100.
5. Tick **Only signed-in members** so that only members of this workspace, signed in, can use it. It starts unticked.
6. Choose **Make link**, then **Copy** it at once: it is not shown again.

The list of links shows each one's **Status**, how often it was **Used**, when it **Expires** and was **Last used**;
**Revoke** stops one.

![A form's Settings with Share by link](form-settings "Settings: what a submission starts, the dataset, and Share by link, off for this workspace")

> **Privacy:** A link without **Only signed-in members** opens the form for anyone who holds it. Do not use one for a
> form that asks for patient or personal data unless your organisation has approved it, and prefer **One (single
> use)** and a short **Works for**.

## Example
Summit Valley keeps sharing by link off: its forms are filled in by staff who are signed in. Rosa Alvarez, the charge
nurse, opens **Nurse triage** from **Get data in › Forms**, records a patient's vitals and pain score, and submits; the
submission is hers by name.
