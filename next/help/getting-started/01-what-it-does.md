The ETL Console takes your organisation's data from where it is, does the work you describe, lets a person check what needs checking, and hands back finished results: reports, dashboards and answers your own systems can read. Everything happens inside your **workspace**, which holds your people, connections, pipelines, runs and bills, and nothing crosses from one workspace to another.

The menu follows that path from left to right (or top to bottom in the sidebar), so the place you need is usually the next group along.

![Home for a workspace administrator](gs-home "Home: what waits for your review, runs that need attention, the way to each part of the console, and this period's figures")

## The four parts
| Part | Menu group | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| Get data in | **Get data in** | Connect storage, databases and APIs (**Connections**), send documents in to be read (**Documents**), and collect answers with **Forms**. |
| Build | **Build** | Describe what a run does in **Pipelines**, when it runs (**Schedules & triggers**), what a model is asked (**Prompts**), and who approves what in **Approval workflows**. The **AI Assistant** can draft a pipeline from a plain description. |
| Run and review | **Run & review** | Follow every run in **Runs**, retry what failed, and decide what waits for a person in **Review**. |
| See results | **See results** | Read **Dashboards**, ask questions in **Analysis**, make signed PDF reports in **Reports**, and convert files or transcribe recordings in **Tools**. |

A workspace administrator also sees **Administration & billing**: the people and their access, sign-in rules, data policies, the bill, settings and the API for your own systems.

## A worked example
At the fictional Summit Valley Health System a referral arrives by email. **Documents** reads its fields, Greta Fischer in health information approves them in **Review**, and the approval starts the admission pipeline. A care plan is drafted by a model and marked DRAFT until Dr. Anika Patel approves it; the discharge summary becomes a signed PDF in **Reports**, and the ward's **Dashboards** show length of stay the next morning. The whole story is told in the tour chapter.

> **Note:** Every person, patient and number in this guide is made up. A model's answer is always a DRAFT for a person to check.

## What you see depends on you
Two people in the same workspace can see different menus. A **workspace administrator** sees every page; a **workspace member** sees the pages their page access profile opens. See [Who sees what](#gs-who-sees-what).
