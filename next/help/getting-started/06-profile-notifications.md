**Your profile** is how you appear across the console: your display name and picture show in the top bar, on approvals you decide and wherever a schedule records who assigned it. Your role and your workspace are set by an administrator and shown here for reference.

![Your profile](gs-profile "Your profile: picture, details, password and appearance")

## Change your details
1. Open your avatar at the top right and choose **Your profile**.
2. Change **Display name**, **Position** (your job title, separate from your permission role) or **Phone** (pick the country, then the number; empty removes it).
3. Choose **Save details**.

Your email address is your sign-in and only an administrator changes it.

## Add or change your picture
1. Under your portrait choose **Add picture** (or **Replace**), or drop an image on the portrait.
2. Under **Position your picture**, move and zoom it into the circle, then choose **Use this picture**.

PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB. Location and camera details are removed from the file. **Remove** takes it away again.

## Change your password
On **Your profile** or **My security**, type your **Current password** and a **New password** that meets the rules shown under it, then choose **Change password**. You are asked for the current one too, so an unlocked screen is not enough to take the account. Changing it signs you out of your other sessions.

## Choose your theme
Under **Appearance** pick **System**, **Light**, **Dark**, **Ocean**, **Midnight** or **High contrast**. It is saved to your account (**Saved to your account**) and follows you to any device. If it could not be saved it reads **Kept on this device only**. The line beneath says your workspace's default; **Use the workspace default** goes back to it.

## Your activity
At the foot of the page, **Your activity** counts your schedules, their runs and failures over the last days and your unread notifications, and lists your recent runs with when they ran and how long they took.

## Where you are signed in
**My security** lists every session open in your name under **Where you're signed in**: the device, address, when it signed in, when it was last active and how. **End session** signs one out at once; **Sign out everywhere else** ends all but this one. Below, **Sign-in history** lists every sign-in to your account, with **Export CSV**.

![My security](gs-my-security "My security: password, two-factor login, where you're signed in and your sign-in history")

## Notifications
The bell in the top bar counts what you have not read: run outcomes, approvals, documents and alerts that are yours.

![The bell's panel](gs-bell "The bell: the latest notifications, Mark all read and View all notifications")

1. Choose the bell to see the latest. Choose one to open what it is about.
2. **Mark all read** clears the count.
3. **View all notifications** opens the **Notifications** page.

On the **Notifications** page you can filter by kind (**All kinds**), show **Unread only**, mark one read, or choose **Open** to go to its run, task or document.

![The Notifications page](gs-notifications "Account › Notifications: every notice addressed to you")

> **Note:** There are no per-person notification settings. What reaches you depends on your work: the schedules in your name, the approvals and reviews assigned to you or your teams, and the alerts that name you or your team. Your welcome email, one-time passwords and account notices go to your email address.
