Home is the first page after you sign in. It puts what needs you at the top, then the way to the rest of the console, then how the work has gone over the period you pick.

![Home](gs-home "Home for an administrator: waiting for review, needs attention, Go to, and the figures")

## What Home shows
From the top:
1. **Waiting for your review**: how many approvals, run results and documents wait for you and your teams, how many are overdue, and **Open Review**. It reads **Nothing is waiting for your review** when you are clear.
2. **Needs attention**: recent runs in which some items failed, with the step and the items. **See failed items** opens the run; **Retry** runs only the failed items again.
3. **Get your first pipeline running**: in a new workspace, the steps to a first completed run, until one has run. **Hide this guide** puts it away.
4. **Go to**: the main parts of the console you may open, each with its one-line hint.
5. The figures: **Schedules**, **Active schedules**, **Running now**, **Last run completed** and **Last run failed**. Each tile opens the list it counts.
6. **Your bill** (administrators): this month so far and what is owed, with **Cost & usage**.
7. The charts: **Schedule status**, **Schedules by last outcome**, **Runs by day** and **Runs by hour**. Choose a cell in **Runs by hour** to see which schedules ran in that hour.

## Choose the period
Use the buttons at the top right: **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **This month**, or **Custom** for your own dates. The line under the title says which days are shown and when the figures were last updated. Refresh reads them again; they also follow runs as they finish.

## What changes with your role
- A **workspace administrator** sees the whole workspace.
- A **workspace member** sees the schedules they made or are assigned (the line under the title says so), and only the cards and links their access opens. A member with no review pages sees no review card.

![Home for a reviewer](gs-home-reviewer "Home for a reviewer: two approvals waiting, and only the parts their profile opens")

> **Note:** Home has no pinned dashboards of its own. Open your boards from **See results › Dashboards**.

## Example
At 07:30 Dr. Anika Patel opens Home and sees **2 waiting for your review** (two care-plan approvals). She chooses **Open Review**, decides both, and Home reads **Nothing is waiting for your review**.
