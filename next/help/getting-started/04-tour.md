Every page of the console has the same frame: the menu on the left, the top bar across the top, and the page itself with its breadcrumb, title and tabs. Learn it once and every part of the product reads the same way.

![The console with the Runs page open](gs-tour "1 The menu · 2 The top bar: notifications, theme, your avatar · 3 The breadcrumb · 4 The page's tabs · 5 Filters · 6 The table")

## The menu
On a wide screen the menu is a sidebar; on a narrower one the same groups sit along the top bar, and on a phone they open from the menu button.

| Group | Entries |
|---|---|
| **Home** | What waits for you, and this period's figures |
| **Get data in** | Connections, Documents, Forms |
| **Build** | Pipelines, AI Assistant, Approval workflows |
| **Run & review** | Runs, Review (with a count of what waits for you) |
| **See results** | Dashboards, Analysis, Reports, Tools |
| **Administration & billing** | Administration, Billing, Settings, Developers (administrators) |

Each entry shows a one-line hint of what it is for. **Collapse** at the foot of the sidebar folds it to icons; choose a group's icon to open the group beside it.

You see only the entries your access opens: see [Who sees what](#gs-who-sees-what).

## The top bar
From right to left:
- **Your avatar** opens the account menu: **Your profile**, **My security**, **Notifications**, **Setup guide** (this help) and **Sign out**. Under your name it shows your role.
- **The theme button** (a sun or a moon) opens the theme menu.
- **The bell** shows how many notifications you have not read; open it for the latest ones.

![The account menu](gs-account-menu "The account menu under your avatar")

## Breadcrumbs, hubs and tabs
The line above a page's title says where you are, as the menu says it: **Run & review › Runs › Latest**. Choose any step to go back to it.

Many entries are a **hub** of tabs. **Runs**, for example, has **Latest**, **By date**, **Analytics**, **Failed** and **Backfills**. Each tab is its own page with its own address, so you can bookmark it or send the link to a colleague who has the same access. A hub shows only the tabs you may open; a hub with a single tab for you shows that page without a tab strip.

## Side panels
Opening a row often slides a panel in from the right (a team, a catalog asset, a run's item) so the list stays where it was. Close it with **Close**, the cross at its top, or the Escape key.

## Tables
Most lists share the same tools:
- a search box and filters above the table; **Clear** removes them all;
- **Columns** to hide or show columns (the choice is remembered on this device);
- sorting by a column's heading;
- a pager at the foot: 50, 100, 150 or 200 rows a page.

Some lists also switch between **Table** and **Cards**.

## Themes
The theme menu offers **System** (Light or Dark, as your device is set), **Light**, **Dark**, **Ocean**, **Midnight** and **High contrast**. Your choice is kept on your account, so it follows you to any device. Until you choose one, the console uses your workspace's default theme.

## Times and dates
Times are shown on the 24-hour clock (14:05, not 2:05 PM) and in your own device's time zone. Recent times may read as "21 min ago" or "26 Oct, 19:27". Billing counts days in your workspace's billing time zone, and its pages say which one.

> **Tip:** Keyboard users: the first Tab on any page offers **Skip to content**, past the top bar.
