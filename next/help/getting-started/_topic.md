## Questions
### I forgot my password. What do I do?
Ask your workspace administrator to reset it; you choose your own at your next sign-in. See [When you cannot sign in](#gs-sign-in--when-you-cannot-sign-in).

### I lost my phone with the authenticator app.
Sign in with one of your recovery codes, or ask an administrator to reset your two-factor sign-in. See [When you lose your phone](#gs-two-factor--when-you-lose-your-phone).

### Why does my colleague see menu entries I don't?
Your role and your page access profile decide your menu. See [Who sees what](#gs-who-sees-what).

### Why was I signed out?
Your workspace signs out sessions that are not used for a while, with a warning a minute before. See [Sign in](#gs-sign-in--when-you-cannot-sign-in).

### Which time zone are the times in?
Your own device's, on the 24-hour clock. Billing days follow your workspace's billing time zone. See [Times and dates](#gs-tour--times-and-dates).

### Can I use a dark screen?
Yes: choose the sun or moon in the top bar and pick **Dark**, **Midnight** or another theme. It follows you to any device. See [Themes](#gs-tour--themes).

### Can I choose which notifications I get?
Not one by one: what reaches you follows the work in your name and your teams'. See [Notifications](#gs-profile--notifications).
