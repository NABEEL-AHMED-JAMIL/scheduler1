Signature picture vs digital signature

The picture is only how the page looks. The digital signature is added to the PDF when the approver approves. It records who approved and when, and any later change to the file breaks it. Test certificate now · trusted certificate on AWS.

Replacing and retiring

Replace makes a new version. Old reports keep the version they used. Retire stops new use. Old reports and their history stay. An asset's page shows where it is used before you retire it; only an asset nothing ever used can be deleted.
SVG, PNG or JPG, up to 10 MB. Each picture is checked by its content and re-encoded. Signatures and stamps need an admin to upload and an approver to approve.
